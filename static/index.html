    Diploma project · CSSE2205 · Software Engineering

    A plant pot that tells you how it feels.

    Sprout is a smart plant pot. Sensors in the pot watch the soil, the light and the air, and the pot turns those numbers into something anyone can read at a glance: one of six faces on its screen, such as HAPPY or THIRSTY, with a web dashboard that explains what is wrong and what to do about it.

    The six faces

    The pot checks these rules from top to bottom and shows the first face that matches. The website uses exactly the same rules, so both always agree.

    Healthy: all is well Normal: worth a look soon Unhealthy: act now Resting: it's dark, the plant sleeps

    How it works

    SensorsSoil, light, temperature, humidity
    ESP32Reads the sensors, drives the screen
    Wi-FiHTTP POST with JSON
    Flask APIApplies the plant rules
    DatabaseReadings and notifications
    Web appDashboard, history, advice
    1. Measure. The pot reads its sensors every few seconds.
    2. Decide. The pot checks six simple rules in order and picks one face, such as HAPPY or THIRSTY.
    3. Send. About every 6 seconds the ESP32 sends the reading to the server over Wi-Fi, or over USB as a backup.
    4. Show. The pot draws the face on its screen. The server checks the reading with the same rules, saves it, and the web app shows live values, history, notifications and advice.

    What is inside

    ESP32

    Microcontroller with built-in Wi-Fi. The brain of the pot.

    Capacitive soil sensor

    Measures soil moisture without metal probes that corrode.

    DHT22

    Air temperature and humidity around the plant.

    BH1750

    Light level in lux, so the pot knows if its spot is bright enough.

    OLED display

    A small screen on the pot that shows the face and the most important reading.

    Flask and a database

    Python backend on the laptop that stores readings and applies the rules.

    The team

    Quick answers

    The pot's face says SLEEPY

    The light is under 10 lux, so the plant is resting. Move the pot closer to a window during the day.

    The website says "Pot offline"

    No reading arrived for 20 seconds. Check that the pot is on, connected to the same Wi-Fi as the laptop, or plugged in by USB.

    Soil moisture always shows 0%

    Make sure the soil sensor is pushed into the soil up to the white line, and that its three wires are firmly connected.

    The pot and website show different faces

    Select "Mint (pot rules)" in the plant menu at the top. Other plant profiles use different ranges.

    The screen shows NO WIFI

    The pot can only join 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi. On an iPhone hotspot, turn on Maximize Compatibility.
